Does the policy generalize?

  1. Two policies run the same evaluator-written instruction on the same robot, in the same room.
  2. The evaluator reviews both rollouts blind and records a preference and each rollout's progress.
  3. Ratings are fitted per embodiment, with uncertainty shown.

Fine-tuning

Does the policy learn a task?

  1. A checkpoint is adapted to one defined task with about 100 demonstrations.
  2. Up to 25 repeated trials are scored against written criteria on a 0–4 scale.
  3. Full success and partial progress are reported separately.
