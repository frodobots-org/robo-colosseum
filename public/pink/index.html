Why RoboColosseum

Most robot policies are evaluated by the people who trained them, in the rooms where they were trained. RoboColosseum moves evaluation into the world: unfamiliar scenes, different robot bodies, blind comparisons by independent evaluators. This has not been done at this scale.

How it works

Two tracks, two questions.

A/B

Open track

Does the policy generalize?

  1. Two policies run the same task on the same robot, in the same room.
  2. The evaluator records a blind preference and each rollout’s progress.
  3. Ratings are fitted per embodiment, with uncertainty shown.
✓/4

Fine-tuning

Does the policy learn a task?

  1. A checkpoint is adapted to one defined task.
  2. 25 repeated trials are scored against written criteria.
  3. Full success and partial progress are reported separately.
