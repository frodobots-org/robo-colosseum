WHY ROBOCOLOSSEUM

Demos are easy.
The world isn’t.

The goal: one public, trustworthy answer to whether a robot policy works outside the lab that built it. Not a demo reel. A record.

01

In the wild

Tasks are run in evaluators’ own kitchens, offices and labs. robot/scene combinations so far. No curated scenes, no favourable lighting.

02

Cross-embodiment

The same policy families on Franka, SO-ARM101, Bimanual YAM, Unitree G1 and more bodies joining. Compared within each body, never pooled across them.

03

Never done at this scale

blind comparisons and scored trials, with Hugging Face, BitRobot and EAIC behind it. Every result links to its recording.
