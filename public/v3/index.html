Leaderboard

Every policy on a body is rated only against the others on that same body. Open-track ratings come from blind pairwise comparisons; fine-tuning results are full-success rates over repeated trials.

Every evaluation

Each dot is one scored evaluation on a real robot. Dots sit near the others from the same scene; colour them by body, track, scene or outcome.

The bodies and the rooms

One protocol, several robot bodies, many unfamiliar rooms. A policy is never scored in the scene it was trained in.
